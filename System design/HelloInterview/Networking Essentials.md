#### Private Address1
192.168.0.0/16

The last "/16" specifies that only first 16 bits (2 bytes), so "192.168" are relevant;

Is non-unique (reusable in different networks), assigned by your router, and used only for communication **within** your local network (LAN).

#### Public Addresses
18.0.0.0/8

Is globally unique, assigned by your Internet Service Provider (ISP), and used for communication **outside** your local network, across the internet.
