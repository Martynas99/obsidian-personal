Currently, I am tech-leading a project to streamline our data ingestion, which is historically one of the biggest hurdles to Risilience's profitability. It started as a hybrid team with an external consultancy, where I was initially brought on as a backend resource. However, I ended up tech-leading the project as we needed stronger alignment between the technology, the actual user needs, and the company's goals.

My contributions started with just writing code and having architecture discussions, but soon expanded to challenging the designs, managing external resources and ensuring what the consultancy built actually fit our use case. For example, one proposed feature included both cell-wise error reporting and in-platform data editing. While editing was a great concept, it introduced complex challenges around data revalidation and lineage. Seeing that this put the delivery timeline at risk I pushed to drop the editing component and keep only the reporting. I realised that users primarily needed to know what and where the errors were, and they were happy fixing them in their spreadsheets and re-uploading, rather than doing it in-platform.

Another technical win on my side was replacing the consultancy's proposed BigQuery setup with a simpler, blob storage-based medallion architecture. BigQuery was overkill, slower for our specific workflow, and added unnecessary complexity. The blob storage approach met all of our target requirements while being far easier to maintain. We now have an internal team fully owning the project, and we are currently finishing its integration into our core platform.

I also have a track record of identifying internal bottlenecks and building tooling to solve them, some of them even became features in our core platform. For example, I collaborated with our in-house modellers to build an input migration pipeline. This allows users of the platform to map their current inputs to the newer versions of the models and removes the friction of adopting our latest science. It started as an internal only tool but now is a core feature in the platform
